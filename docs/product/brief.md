# Product brief

## Problem

People can spend more time browsing Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, and other
streaming services than watching a movie. Large catalogues, fragmented
availability, and too many similar choices create analysis paralysis.

## Product promise

Movie Night helps a person or group move from “we want to watch something” to a
confident choice quickly, without reproducing another endless catalogue.

## Initial audience

- Individuals who want a good movie with minimal effort.
- Couples, families, and groups trying to agree on one choice.
- Viewers whose options are constrained by time, mood, genre, or available
  streaming services.

## MVP journey

1. Ask one visible question: “What are you in the mood for tonight?”
2. Interpret the free-text answer into validated movie criteria.
3. Play a short, accessible cinema-countdown transition while the request runs.
4. Reveal one highly rated recommendation with UK certification and
   availability information.
5. Let the viewer try the next film in a finite eight-film queue without
   re-entering the prompt.
6. When the queue is exhausted, broaden the quality policy in a controlled
   order without repeating a referenced or shown film.

## Experience principles

1. Reduce decisions rather than expose every possible option.
2. Ask only questions that materially improve the recommendation.
3. Make a small number of recommendations easy to compare and trust.
4. Explain why a recommendation fits without overwhelming the user.
5. Provide graceful paths when preferences conflict or no result is available.
6. Treat accessibility, privacy, and mobile use as first-class requirements.

## Initial success questions

- How quickly can a user reach a choice they are happy to watch?
- What proportion of recommendation sessions end with a selection?
- How often do people restart, reject every result, or abandon the flow?
- Can users understand why each movie was recommended?

## Open questions

- Is the first release for individuals, groups, or both?
- Is sign-in or saved history necessary for the core experience?
- How will recommendation quality be evaluated without encouraging more
  browsing?

## Confirmed MVP constraints

- Default region: United Kingdom.
- Availability: live TMDB/JustWatch information; subscription, free,
  ad-supported, rental, and purchase options are all acceptable.
- Adult films may be recommended, but the UK rating must be prominent.
- The project is non-commercial. Revisit TMDB licensing before any commercial
  use.
- Accounts and durable recommendation history are out of scope.
